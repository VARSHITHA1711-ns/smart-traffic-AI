⚡ Dynamic Green Allocation Formula

The AI continuously computes Passenger Car Units (PCU) for Indian traffic (2-Wheeler: 0.5, Car: 1.0, Auto: 1.0, Bus: 3.0, Truck: 3.0). Signal duration is determined by:

Green_Duration = Clamp(MinGreen + (Density% / 100) * (MaxGreen - MinGreen) + (Queue * 1.2) + (WaitTime * 0.15), 15s, 75s)
